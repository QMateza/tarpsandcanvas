Who we are

Our Story & Leadership

Founded in 1994, Tarpaulin & Canvas has grown into the Eastern Cape’s leading manufacturer of custom PVC and canvas products. Now owned by Nande Urban Hub (Pty) Ltd—a black female-owned manufacturing company—we continue to build a strong, sustainable brand backed by nearly three decades of industry expertise and a reputation for quality.

Local Manufacturing Excellence

With almost 30 years of experience, we operate from a 1000sqm ISO9001:2015-certified factory in Braelyn, East London. Here, our skilled team manufactures high-quality products and provides reliable on-site installations and repairs. From tension curtains and picking bags to boat covers and blinds, every item is custom-made with precision.

Innovation That Drives Results

In 2018, we engineered and patented the first tarpaulin-based portable water tanker system—expanding our capabilities into agriculture, transport, and water storage. This innovation reflects our commitment to efficiency, durability, and solutions that solve real-world challenges across multiple industries.

Growing Across Africa

Our patented Porta Tank system is exported to countries like Tanzania and Seychelles through trusted distribution partners. As we expand across Africa, our long-term goal is to become Southern Africa’s preferred PVC manufacturer—delivering custom solutions, excellent service, and world-class products to every customer.
